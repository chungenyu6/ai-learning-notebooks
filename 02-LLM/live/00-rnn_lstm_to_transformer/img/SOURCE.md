# Figure sources

All `15_*.png` figures are copied unchanged from
[rasbt/machine-learning-book](https://github.com/rasbt/machine-learning-book/tree/main/ch15/figures),
Chapter 15 of *Machine Learning with PyTorch and Scikit-Learn* by Sebastian Raschka, Yuxi (Hayden) Liu and Vahid Mirjalili (Packt, 2022).
They are distributed under the MIT License included here as `LICENSE-machine-learning-book.txt`.

`../data/1268-0.txt` is *The Mysterious Island* by Jules Verne from Project Gutenberg (EBook #1268, public domain in the USA), as used in the same chapter.

`ch03_03.webp` – `ch03_06.webp` are figures from Chapter 3 of *Build a Large Language Model (From Scratch)* by Sebastian Raschka (Manning, 2024),
downloaded unchanged from the author's site (`https://sebastianraschka.com/images/LLMs-from-scratch-images/ch03_compressed/`) as used in
[rasbt/LLMs-from-scratch](https://github.com/rasbt/LLMs-from-scratch). The repository's Apache-2.0 license explicitly excludes the book and its images;
copyright remains with the author/publisher. Included here for educational use with attribution.
